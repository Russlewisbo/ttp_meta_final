```
5. Selection:                37.8%
6. Comparability:            27.0%  ← Most problematic
```

### Most Problematic Domain: Comparability

**Issue:** Inadequate adjustment for confounders

**Common unmeasured confounders:**
- Disease severity scores (APACHE II, SOFA, Pitt bacteremia score)
- Appropriateness of empiric antibiotic therapy
- Time to effective therapy
- Source control adequacy and timing
- Comorbidity burden (Charlson index)

**Clinical significance:** TTP may partially proxy for disease severity, so observed associations could be confounded by baseline patient characteristics.

## Clinical Interpretation

### Findings Appear Robust Despite Moderate Risk

**Supporting evidence:**
1. **Consistency:** TTP-mortality association consistent across studies with varying risk levels
2. **Meta-regression:** Overall risk of bias did NOT significantly modify pooled estimates (95% CrI for ROB coefficient included zero)
3. **Sensitivity analysis:** Excluding high/critical risk studies (n=2) did not materially change pooled OR
4. **Bayesian methods:** Robust estimation prevented undue influence of outliers
5. **Biological plausibility:** TTP reflects bacterial burden, a known prognostic factor

**Remaining concerns:**
1. Residual confounding by disease severity
2. All observational studies (no RCTs)
3. Heterogeneous TTP measurement protocols
4. Possible publication bias (see funnel plot analysis)

**Conclusion:** The moderate risk of bias is acknowledged but does not invalidate findings. The TTP-mortality association appears genuine, though magnitude may be partially confounded. Future prospective studies with comprehensive severity adjustment would strengthen the evidence base.

## Assessment Tool

**Modified Newcastle-Ottawa Scale for Prognostic Factor Studies**

**Six Domains Assessed:**
1. **Selection:** Representativeness, sample size
2. **Comparability:** Confounder adjustment
3. **Outcome:** Objective assessment, follow-up
4. **Statistical Analysis:** Appropriate methods
5. **Attrition:** Loss to follow-up documentation
6. **Prognostic Measurement:** Objective TTP measurement

**Overall risk determined by:**
- Pattern across domains
- Severity of concerns
- Potential impact on results
